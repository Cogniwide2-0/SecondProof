BA
01

Business Analyst

Understands the system

Drafts a BRD in your template, then runs the gap analysis against the live app. Every requirement links to its source.

ReadsWord, PDF, Confluence, a brief or the app URLMakesBRD draft, gap analysis, open questions

Never invents a requirement without a source, or approves its own work.

TD
02

Test Designer

Designs the coverage

Happy paths, negatives, boundaries, risk-weighted depth. Reuses the cases you already have.

ReadsApproved BRD, risk, coverage target, your casesMakesTest pack draft, coverage map

Never designs from an unapproved BRD, or deletes your cases.

QA leadHands to Tester →AI · generate onceDuplicates, coverage · 0
TE
03

Tester

Runs the approved plan

Build verification on every new build, across apps, workstation, AI and data. Screenshots, logs and replays for every step.

ReadsNew build, approved plan, environment, labelled dataMakesResults with evidence per step

Never types a password or one-time code, changes the plan or touches production.

QA lead approves the planHands to Verifier →Runs, retries · 0Sign-ins to a person
VR
04

Verifier

Second-agent verification

Re-runs every failure on its own and sorts it: product bug, test issue, environment or data, with confidence.

ReadsFailure, evidence, run historyMakesReproduced or not, category, confidence

Never hides a failure, or decides one for a person.

Seen by a QA engineer in the inboxHands to Defect Manager →Re-run · 0AI · small-model review
DM
05

Defect Manager

Turns failures into defects

Drafts defects with steps, evidence and severity. Links duplicates and syncs approved ones to Jira or Azure DevOps.

ReadsVerified failures, tracker historyMakesDefect drafts, duplicate links

Never files to your tracker before approval, or closes a defect.

QA lead or engineerHands to your tracker, then a re-run →AI · new failures onlyDuplicate rules · 0
AE
06

Automation Engineer

Write once, run free

Turns passed tests into scripts in your repository. Rules repair broken locators first, AI only when rules can’t decide.

ReadsPassed tests, your test repositoryMakesScripts and repairs as pull requests

Never merges its own pull request, or quarantines a test.

Your code reviewersHands to the regression suite →AI · generate onceRuns, rule repair · 0
PE
07

Performance Engineer

Load with safety stops

Load scenarios from the BRD and your traffic profile, with a ramp, an abort threshold and a stop button.

ReadsBRD, traffic profile, agreed limitsMakesScenario, p95 results, bottleneck notes

Never runs above the agreed limit without approval, or loads production.

Performance leadHands to Release Manager →AI · design, explainLoad runs · 0
RM
08

Release Manager

Prepares the decision

Checks results against every quality gate, assembles the Evidence Pack and prepares the go/no-go with a plain risk summary.

ReadsResults, defects, waivers, gate rulesMakesGate status, Evidence Pack, go/no-go request

Never signs a go/no-go, or waives a check.

Your named approvers signHands to Go / No-go →Gate metrics · 0AI · risk summary
