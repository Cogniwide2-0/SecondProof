Inside every pack

01 · READY TESTSProcesses already writtenPick the ones you use. Adapted only where your setup differs; a QA lead approves.
02 · READY COMPONENTSHow to test the platformDrive its screens, check results through its APIs, build its test data. 0 AI credits per run.
03 · CHANGE IMPACTOnly what the change touchesThe smallest safe set of tests per transport, update or release. Roles and access re-checked.

How a pack runs

  1. OverviewWhere it stands, next change
  2. Ready testsPick your processes
  3. AdaptEngines find differences; AI drafts once
  4. ApproveQA lead. Nothing runs before
  5. Pack runImpacted tests, step evidence
  6. TriageA person decides each failure
  7. Go / No-goInto the signed decision

Three failure types

Expected platform changeOn the vendor’s change listA tool finds it, the AI explains it. Accept the new result or raise a defect.
Defect in your setupYour configuration breaksDraft a defect for your team, or waive for this run with a reason.
Test-data problemA refresh wiped the dataA known pattern, matched by a tool, no AI. Rebuild and rerun.
