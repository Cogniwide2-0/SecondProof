Trust and security

Built for banks, governments and regulated releases.

Designed in from day one. Certifications are targets, and we say so.

Live app only. No code access.We test the running app and its integrations. Your code stays with your team.
SSO and SCIMEntra ID, Okta, Google · SAML or OIDC · passkeys. Email sign-in for small teams.
Roles and permissionsEvery menu, button and record follows your role. Admins set each role.
Credentials in the vaultEntered once, used by reference. Agents never see them.
Human-only sign-in stepsNational ID, OTP and card steps go to a person. The run waits, then resumes.
Tamper-evident auditCreate, approve, waive, sign, export: who, role, before, after, source. Never editable.
Data residencyRegion per workspace, including UAE and KSA. The AI model region follows.
PDPL and GDPRDesigned to align with UAE and KSA data protection law and GDPR.
SOC 2 and ISO 27001 TargetsOn our roadmap. Status shared openly in your security review.
Test-data maskingAll test data labelled. Personal data masked or synthetic outside production.
